# The 75-Day Career Upgrade Challenge

**From IT support or sysadmin to cloud, SRE, platform or DevOps engineer, in 30 minutes a day, with AI as your study partner.**

> ### Explore first. Specialise later.
> You don't need Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitHub Actions, pipelines and Generative AI all at once. Nobody uses all of them every day. But you can't choose the path that fits you until you've tried each one. So for 75 days, we explore. Then you pick, and go deep.

## How it works
- **30 minutes a day, 5 days a week.** One longer "build" session at the weekend. One day to catch up or rest.
- **Weekly goals, not daily punishment.** Missed a few days? You're not out. Finish the week's goal when you can.
- **Every week starts with *why this skill matters* and *who can skip it*.** Skipping a week you've honestly ruled out is allowed.
- **Three checkpoints** (weeks 5, 9 and 11) with small projects and a self-check: Starter → Working → Intermediate. Nobody becomes an "expert" in 75 days, and we don't pretend otherwise.
- **Week 11: pick your path.** Use what you enjoyed and your self-check results to choose where to go deep.
