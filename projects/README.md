# Projects

Three projects become your portfolio. Each lives in **your own** public GitHub repo, so you can link it on your CV.

| Project | Opens | You prove | App |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Checkpoint 1: app on Kubernetes](checkpoint-1-kubernetes/) | Week 5 | You can run a real 2-tier app on Kubernetes the production-ready way | [`apps/notes-app`](../apps/notes-app/) |
| [Checkpoint 2: end to end](checkpoint-2-end-to-end/) | Week 9 | A pipeline tests, builds and deploys your app onto infrastructure that Terraform creates | [`apps/notes-app`](../apps/notes-app/) |
| [Capstone: AI app to production](capstone-ai-app/) | Week 11 | You can run an AI app like any other workload: safely, observably, repeatably | [`apps/error-explainer`](../apps/error-explainer/) |

**Everything runs free on your laptop** (Docker + kind) and in free GitHub Actions minutes. No cloud bill is needed. If you have an Azure free account, each brief has an optional cloud extension.

**How to score yourself:** each brief has a Starter / Working / Intermediate table.
- Be honest. The goal is to know where you stand, not to collect ticks.
- Put your level and repo link in the *Projects* tab of your tracker.

**Solutions** are shared after each checkpoint week, in the weekly email. Try first. Getting stuck and getting unstuck is the learning.
